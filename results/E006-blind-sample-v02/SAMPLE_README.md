# E006: blind sample of verbatim v0.2 misses

`sample_blind.json` has 63 quotes from E004 reports (OpenAI 48, Perplexity 15) that verbatim v0.2, after the E005
fixes, does not find (NOT_FOUND or UNCERTAIN). These are all quotes of at least 6 words from reports that gave nothing
to the first sample (the 58 in E004). Each item has the quote, its report paragraph, its own links (`urls`) and every
link of the paragraph (`block_urls`). It carries no verdicts, doubts or closest passages.

`sample_store/` holds the snapshots verbatim read for these links: `index.json` maps URL to sha256, and the files
are in `snapshots/`. Labelling against these bytes measures the tool and not page drift.
`verbatim text <url> --store sample_store` prints a page's text.

verbatim's verdicts are sealed: `verdicts_sealed_sha256` in the sample is the sha256 of `verdicts_sealed.json`
on branch `e006-sealed-verdicts` of tsyyan/lab. Do not read that branch before your labels are committed.

Generated by `experiments/E004-deep-research-quotes/scripts/sample_blind.py` (seed 2026).
